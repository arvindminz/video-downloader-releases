Video Downloader is a high-performance native Android media downloader powered by yt-dlp and FFmpeg.
🌟 Key Features\n
•Multi-Resolution Video & Audio: Download videos from 4K (2160p) down to 144p, or extract MP3/M4A audio in high quality.
•Realtime Streaming Playlist Downloader: Instantly stream and batch-download entire YouTube/site playlists without waiting.
•Built-in ExoPlayer Video Player: Watch videos directly in-app with quality toggles, CC subtitles, speed controls, background audio playback, and related video feeds.
•Dual Queue & Downloads Manager:
◦Queue Tab: View multi-threaded chunk progress per download segment with a 1-tap "Go to downloads" option.
◦Downloads Tab: Active music conversions stay pinned at the top of your list. Includes a 3-dot action menu for Share, Delete, Rename, Convert to Music, and Other Resolutions.
◦Fast-Draggable Scrollbar: Touch and hold the right-edge scrollbar to fast-scroll through long file lists.
•Web-Based In-App Update System: Get instant notification banners and 1-tap updates whenever a new version is released.
